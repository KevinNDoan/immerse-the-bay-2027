## Known problems

- `LineRenderer.textureScale` is assumed to exist in Unity 6. If the compile fails on it, delete that one line in `UpdateLines`; the line will tile once per metre.
- Star layers use `SpriteRenderer` with the `Zap/Textured` material. Sorting relies on `sortingOrder` plus small depth offsets. This is not checked in the headset.
- `Packages/packages-lock.json` and `.vsconfig` were changed before this session. Leave them out of design-lane commits.
